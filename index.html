About AI-Cell

Innovation • Artificial Intelligence • Collaboration

What is AI-Cell?

AI-Cell is a student-focused technology club under the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at KPRIT. It provides a platform to learn, experiment and collaborate in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, Data Science, Robotics and emerging technologies.

Our Vision

To create a community of technically curious students who transform innovative ideas into practical AI-powered solutions through projects, workshops, hackathons and collaborative activities.
